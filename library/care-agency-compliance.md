## The Person
Sharon has run her care agency for 8 years, building it from 12 staff to 55 through sheer force of will and genuine care for her clients. She's brilliant with people, terrible with email, and has never once opened a spreadsheet without swearing. She named her AI assistant. It's a team member now, not a tool.

## Core Pain
CQC compliance is a ticking clock. Miss a deadline, fail an audit, lose your registration. Meanwhile, 55 staff means daily scheduling conflicts, last-minute sickness cover, and a WhatsApp group that never stops pinging. Sharon was spending her evenings doing admin that shouldn't need her, stealing hours from the growth work that actually moves the business forward.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- CQC policy updates that need acknowledging before a deadline nobody tracks
- Shift swaps that create compliance gaps (wrong qualifications covering wrong clients)
- Staff training certificate renewals that expire silently
- Care plan review dates that slip past until an auditor asks
- New starter onboarding paperwork that sits incomplete for weeks
- Incident reports that need follow-up actions logged within 48 hours

## How Navi Helps
Navi tracks every CQC compliance deadline and sends Sharon reminders before they become emergencies. When staff request shift swaps, Navi checks qualifications against client requirements before confirming. Policy lookups that used to mean digging through folders now take seconds. That's 7 hours a week back, 28 hours a month reinvested into growing the agency.

## Example Morning Message
> Morning Sharon. Two things before your 9am. Keisha's DBS check expires in 12 days — I've drafted a reminder for her. And there's a shift conflict tomorrow night: Marcus is covering Bay Lodge but he hasn't completed his medication administration refresher. Want me to find qualified cover or reschedule his training this week?

**Sharon:** Can you check if we're compliant on fire safety training for the Elm House team?
**Navi:** Elm House team: 8 of 9 staff are current. David Chen's fire safety certificate expired 3 weeks ago. CQC requires all staff to be current. Want me to book him onto the next available session and flag this in the compliance log?
