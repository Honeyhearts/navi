## The Person
Marco has run a taco truck in LA for 4 years. He works 5 locations a week — two lunch spots near office parks, one Thursday farmers market, weekend festivals. His food is excellent and his Instagram following has built organically to 8,400. But he runs everything himself: cooking, driving, social media, catering quotes, city permit tracking. He's at capacity and can't grow without drowning.

## Core Pain
People follow the truck on Instagram because they want to know where Marco is today. He forgets to post until he's already set up and cooking. Catering inquiries come in via DM and email and he answers them days late, then loses the booking. Ingredient reorders happen reactively — he runs out of something mid-service and has to improvise or disappoint customers.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Posting his location for the day (before service, not during)
- Responding to catering inquiries within 24 hours
- Tracking which ingredients are running low before they run out
- Following up with catering leads who went quiet
- Managing the city permit calendar (different rules per location)
- Keeping the catering menu and pricing updated across his website, Instagram, and DMs

## How Navi Helps
Navi handles the announcement layer — it knows Marco's schedule and posts his location each morning. It drafts catering inquiry responses, surfaces inventory reorder alerts, and keeps his pipeline honest so Marco can focus on the food.

> Good morning Marco. Today: Westwood lunch spot, 11am-2pm. Your location post isn't up yet — want me to send the standard "We're at [location] today" post to Instagram? Also: two catering inquiries came in this weekend — one for a corporate lunch in June (80 people), one for a birthday party in May. Both haven't been replied to. And based on last week's volume, you're probably low on guajillo chiles.
