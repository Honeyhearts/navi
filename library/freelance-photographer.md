## The Person
Carlos has been shooting weddings and portraits for 7 years. He's talented, his Instagram converts, and he's booked solid May through October. The business side of it all — contracts, invoices, gallery delivery, the 40+ emails that accompany every wedding — is where things fall apart.

## Core Pain
Money is often late because he forgets to follow up on invoices. Galleries sit on his hard drive for two weeks after a wedding because he hasn't had time to export and send them. Leads email, he responds three days late, they've already booked someone else.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Following up on unpaid invoices (especially retainers)
- Responding to inquiries fast enough to win the booking
- Delivering galleries on time
- Sending questionnaires before shoots
- Collecting testimonials after shoots

## How Navi Helps
Navi runs his client pipeline — surfaces overdue invoices, drafts follow-ups, reminds him when a gallery is overdue, sends post-shoot requests for reviews. He checks in once a day and the business side mostly handles itself.

## Example Morning Message
> Morning Carlos. Two things need attention today: the $1,400 final payment from the Garcia wedding is 9 days overdue — want me to send a friendly reminder? Also the Patel portrait gallery was due yesterday. Do you need more editing time, or should I send the delivery email with a short delay notice?

## Example Conversation
**Carlos:** Send the Garcia reminder, keep it casual
**Navi:** Draft: "Hey Maria! Just a friendly heads up — the final balance of $1,400 is due for your November 12th wedding. You can pay via the link in your original invoice. Thanks so much, can't wait to share the final photos soon!" Good to go?
