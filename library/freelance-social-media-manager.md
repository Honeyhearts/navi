## The Person
Destiny manages social media for 7 small business clients — a salon, a restaurant, a boutique gym, two service businesses, and two personal brands. She creates content, schedules posts, monitors engagement, and reports monthly. She does this alone. The actual content creation she loves. The client management — approvals, reporting, revisions, check-ins — is a second job that runs alongside the creative work every day.

## Core Pain
Every client has a different approval process. One client approves via DM, one via email, one insists on a Google Doc. Content sits unapproved for days, then the client asks why things aren't posted. Monthly reports take her a full day to compile manually from each platform's native analytics, formatted differently per client.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Following up on content approvals that have gone cold
- Knowing which client's content is due, approved, or stalled at a glance
- Sending monthly reports before clients have to ask for them
- Checking in with clients before renewal conversations
- Onboarding new clients with a consistent brief and intake process
- Tracking hours per client to know if she's undercharging

## How Navi Helps
Navi is Destiny's agency dashboard — it tracks where every client's content is in the pipeline, surfaces approval delays, reminds her when reports are due, and drafts the client check-in communications so she spends her time making content, not managing people.
