## The Person
Derek has been roofing for 12 years and went independent 5 years ago. He runs a 3-man crew and does mostly residential — storm damage, full replacements, repairs. He's good enough that word of mouth keeps him busy. He uses JobNimbus because someone told him to, QuickBooks for invoices, and a group text for crew coordination. None of them talk to each other and he touches all three every day.

## Core Pain
Leads come in and he can't always call back within 5 minutes — he's on a roof. Industry data shows roofing leads contacted within 5 minutes are 21x more likely to convert than those contacted after 30 minutes (RooferMarketing, 2025). Derek is calling back in 3 hours. He also writes estimates and sends them, then never follows up — the average roofing lead requires 5-7 follow-up attempts but most contractors stop at 2 (RenoGrove, 2026). Derek stops at 0.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Calling back new leads before someone else does (missed calls cost $1,500-2,000 each)
- Following up on sent estimates that have gone quiet (50% of leads don't convert due to poor follow-up)
- Sending a fast, credible first-response when he can't pick up the phone
- Scheduling crew jobs and communicating daily start times and addresses
- Tracking material orders and flagging when a delivery is late and could stall a job
- Collecting the signed contract and deposit before mobilizing the crew

## How Navi Helps
Navi is Derek's front office. When a lead comes in and he's on a roof, Navi sends an immediate, professional response that buys time and sets expectations. It runs the follow-up sequence on sent estimates — the 2-day, 5-day, 10-day touches that Derek never does. It tracks material orders and pings him before a delivery issue becomes a scheduling problem.

## Example Morning Message
> Good morning Derek. Three things: new lead came in at 7:42am — Paul Simmons, storm damage, NW OKC. You haven't called back yet. I sent a holding message last night: "Hi Paul — got your message, will call you this morning to set up a free inspection. Thanks for reaching out." Two estimates are in follow-up range: the Henderson job (sent 6 days ago, no response) and the Castillo replacement (sent 11 days ago). Want me to send touches? And the shingles for the Merritt job are showing delayed delivery — originally Thursday, now Monday.

Still very interested in doing this project — is this something you're still moving forward with?"
