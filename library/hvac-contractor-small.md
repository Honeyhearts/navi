## The Person
Luis started his HVAC company 7 years ago. He now has 3 technicians and a van. He's the best tech on the team and also the dispatcher, estimator, office manager, and bookkeeper. ServiceTitan's research shows HVAC techs cite "having to provide historical job and equipment information for each job" and "spending hours converting estimates to invoices" as top operational pain points. Luis lives all of these simultaneously.

## Core Pain
Phoenix summers mean emergency calls overwhelming his scheduling. A tech finishes a job and Luis has to manually figure out the next dispatch. Estimates sit unconverted because he's in the field. Invoices go unpaid because no one is following up. He has QuickBooks, a whiteboard, and a group text with his techs. None of them talk to each other.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Dispatching the right tech to the right job based on location and skill
- Converting completed estimates into invoices before the window closes
- Following up on unpaid invoices (especially larger installs)
- Sending maintenance reminders to residential clients (great for repeat business)
- Tracking equipment history per address for faster service calls
- Seasonal demand prep — getting ahead of summer surge with maintenance outreach

## How Navi Helps
Navi coordinates the office side of Luis's operation. It tracks open estimates, flags unpaid invoices, drafts dispatch messages to techs, and runs the seasonal maintenance outreach so Luis can stay in the field without the back office collapsing.

## Example Morning Message
> Good morning Luis. Today: 6 jobs across 3 techs. Miguel is closest to the Thornton emergency — want me to text him the address and job details? Two estimates from this week haven't been converted to invoices yet ($1,200 Reyes, $3,400 Birchfield install). And 47 residential clients from last summer haven't had maintenance outreach yet — good week to start since the heat is coming.
