## The Person
Priya built her events company from scratch after leaving a corporate marketing role 4 years ago. She's got great taste, a solid reputation, and enough inbound inquiries to be busy. The problem is she's so busy running events that the next event doesn't get booked until it's too late. She knows she's leaving money on the table. She just can't get to the inbox fast enough.

## Core Pain
Warm leads go cold. Someone fills out her contact form on Tuesday, Priya sees it Thursday, responds Friday, and by then they've already booked a competitor who replied in 20 minutes. It's not that she doesn't care. It's that she's physically at a venue setting up chair covers while her phone buzzes with inquiries she can't answer.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Contact form submissions that sit unread for 48-72 hours
- Instagram DM inquiries that get buried under story replies
- Follow-up emails after initial calls that never get sent
- Quote requests that need pricing lookups across vendors before responding
- Post-event thank you messages and review requests
- Repeat client check-ins that could generate referrals

## How Navi Helps
Navi captures every lead the moment it comes in, sends a personalized follow-up within minutes (not a generic autoresponder, a real response that matches Priya's tone), and drafts quotes based on event type and her standard pricing. By the time Priya checks her phone after a setup, the lead is already warm and waiting for her personal touch, not cold and gone.

## Example Morning Message
> Morning Priya. 3 new inquiries overnight. One is a corporate Christmas party for 120, budget looks strong — I've already responded with availability and your portfolio link. The other two are birthday parties, smaller scope. I've sent initial responses to all three. Your follow-up call list today: Sarah Chen (wedding tasting, booked for 2pm), and Marcus from Bloom & Co wants to discuss their summer series. Quotes attached for both.

## Example Conversation
**Priya:** Just got off a call with a bride who wants a garden party for 80 in June. Can you put together a quote?
**Navi:** Based on your garden party package for 80 guests: venue dressing £1,800, catering coordination £600, florals estimate £1,200, staffing for 6 hours £900. Total estimate: £4,500 before supplier markups. Want me to send this as a formal quote with your standard terms, or adjust anything first?
