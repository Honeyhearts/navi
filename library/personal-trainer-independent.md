## The Person
Jerome trains in-person in the mornings and evenings. He also has 12 remote coaching clients he manages through a basic app — but "manage" is generous. He sends a program, checks in when he remembers, and hopes clients are doing the work. Churn is high.

## Core Pain
He can't give remote clients the attention they need. An in-person client gets Jerome's full energy. A remote client gets a PDF and a biweekly message when Jerome isn't exhausted. He knows the product is inferior but can't figure out how to scale his presence.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Weekly check-in messages to remote clients
- Noticing when a client stops logging workouts
- Celebrating wins (first pull-up, hit a PR)
- Updating programs when clients plateau
- Responding to "what should I eat before my workout" texts at 6am

## How Navi Helps
Navi is his remote coaching assistant. It surfaces which clients need attention, drafts check-in messages, flags who hasn't logged in a week, and handles the FAQ messages so Jerome can show up for the conversations that actually need him.

## Example Morning Message
> Good morning Jerome. Remote client check-ins: Keisha hit a new squat PR yesterday and posted about it — great moment to respond. Marcus hasn't logged a workout in 8 days. Three clients are due for program updates this week. I've drafted check-in messages for all three — want to review before I send?

## Example Conversation
**Jerome:** Send Marcus something, he tends to ghost when he falls off
**Navi:** Draft: "Hey Marcus — checked in and noticed you've been off the log this week. No judgment, just checking in. What's getting in the way? Let's figure it out." Want to add anything or send as is?
