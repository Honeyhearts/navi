## The Person
Patricia has run a solo bookkeeping practice for 8 years. She has 22 active clients, mostly small businesses and sole proprietors. She's good at the work. The practice management — tracking who owes her what, who she's waiting on, what's due when — is a second job she handles with a homemade spreadsheet and a prayer. Research shows 45% of accounting professionals want to automate repetitive tasks like document chasing and data entry (Financial Cents, 2024). Patricia is in that 45%.

## Core Pain
Clients don't send their receipts, bank statements, or payroll reports on time. She has to ask. Then ask again. During tax season she's chasing 15 clients simultaneously while also doing the actual work. Missed deadlines reflect on her even when the client caused the delay.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Sending document request reminders to slow clients
- Tracking which monthly closes are complete vs. outstanding
- Notifying clients when their books are ready to review
- Following up on invoices for her own services
- Onboarding new clients (checklist, document collection, access setup)
- Catching clients who are about to hit a quarterly deadline

## How Navi Helps
Navi runs Patricia's practice coordination — it knows which clients are behind, surfaces who needs a nudge, drafts the follow-up messages, and tracks the close calendar so nothing slips through during the heavy months.

## Example Morning Message
> Good morning Patricia. March closes: 14 of 22 complete. Outstanding: Rivera Construction hasn't sent payroll reports (due 5 days ago), Bloom Salon hasn't sent March receipts. Two others are close but need a final bank statement. Want me to draft requests for all four? Also: your own invoice to Westside Cafe is 18 days unpaid.

## Example Conversation
**Patricia:** Yes send to all four, and send the Westside invoice reminder — that one's been an issue before
**Navi:** Sending document requests to the four clients now, each personalized with what's specifically missing. For Westside: "Hi Marco — just flagging that invoice #47 from March 12th ($420) is still outstanding. Happy to resend the invoice if it got buried. Let me know!" Good?
