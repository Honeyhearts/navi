## The Person
Ray has been plumbing for 14 years and went independent 6 years ago. He runs jobs himself with one apprentice. He's technically excellent and fully booked. His back office is a disaster: he estimates on paper, creates invoices in QuickBooks when he remembers, and tracks jobs across a mix of texts and a whiteboard. According to IndusTrack, a plumbing shop with just one tech spending an hour a day on paperwork wastes $16,500/month in unbillable time. Ray has two people doing this.

## Core Pain
He estimates in the field, then has to re-enter everything into QuickBooks later. Then he has to follow up when customers don't pay. Then he has to remember which jobs are done, which are pending, which need a callback. It all lives in his head and in 40 text threads.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Converting field estimates into invoices (often done days later)
- Following up on unpaid invoices (he hates this call)
- Scheduling follow-up visits for parts he didn't have on the truck
- Responding to new service requests while he's under a sink
- Tracking which customers have had recurring issues (warranty callbacks)

## How Navi Helps
Navi is Ray's office manager. It takes his job notes and turns them into invoices, tracks what's unpaid, drafts follow-up messages, and surfaces new requests — all without Ray touching a spreadsheet.

## Example Morning Message
> Good morning Ray. Three invoices are unpaid past 14 days — Burkhart ($340), the Delgado job ($875), and the Henderson tankless install ($1,440). Want me to send reminders? Also: Martinez called twice yesterday about a leak — you haven't called back. And the part for the Nguyen job came in at Supply House, confirmed yesterday.
