## The Person
Vanessa has been doing hair for 9 years. She rents a booth, books through Instagram DMs and word of mouth, and has a loyal clientele. But no-shows bleed her every week. Industry data shows salon no-show rates average 15-30%, and at $85-120 per appointment, a salon with 200 monthly bookings loses $2,550-$5,100 a month to no-shows (Vocaly AI, 2024). Vanessa's running the same math at a smaller scale and feeling every missed appointment.

## Core Pain
She texts reminders herself, but she's doing hair when she should be sending them, so they go out late or not at all. She's tried Calendly but her clients aren't tech-forward and the link adds friction. She's tried Square but doesn't understand the dashboard. She needs something that just works without a setup wall.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Sending reminders 48 hours and 2 hours before appointments
- Following up when someone no-shows (should they rebook?)
- Reaching out to clients who haven't been in 8+ weeks
- Responding to Instagram DM booking requests before they go to another stylist
- Asking satisfied clients for Google reviews

## How Navi Helps
Navi runs her client communication layer — automated reminders at the right intervals, rebooking nudges for clients who've drifted, and first-response to new DMs so Vanessa doesn't lose a potential client while her hands are occupied.

## Example Morning Message
> Good morning Vanessa. You have 6 appointments today. Reminders went out last night to all 6 — 4 confirmed, 2 haven't responded (Monique at 11am and DeShawn at 3pm). Want me to send a same-day reminder to those two now? Also: Keisha hasn't been in since January — 10 weeks. Good time to reach out?
