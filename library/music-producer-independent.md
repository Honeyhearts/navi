## The Person
Andre has been producing independently for 8 years. He works with indie artists, does sync licensing, and occasionally takes label projects. At any given time he has 4-8 active projects. He tracks everything in his head, which worked until it didn't.

## Core Pain
He misses check-in windows. An artist sends stems and waits two weeks for feedback. A sync opportunity deadline slips by because it was in his email. He's not disorganized — he's operating at a scale where informal tracking breaks down.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Responding to artist check-ins ("where are we on the mix?")
- Tracking which projects are waiting on him vs. waiting on the artist
- Deadline reminders for sync submissions
- Following up with artists who owe him stems or revisions
- Invoicing after project milestones

## How Navi Helps
Navi is his project board that talks. It knows where every project stands, surfaces who's waiting and who's been waiting too long, drafts status updates, and reminds him about deadlines before they're a problem.

## Example Morning Message
> Morning Andre. Three projects need your attention today: the Lena EP mix has been with you 8 days — she last checked in Tuesday. The Fontaine single sync deadline is Friday at noon. And Marcus sent the revised reference track last night — your call when you've got a listen. Want me to send Lena an update?
