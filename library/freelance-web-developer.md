## The Person
Priya has been freelancing as a web developer for 5 years. She specializes in React and Shopify builds, has good clients, and charges solid rates. The business side is a constant friction source. Research shows freelancers waste 5+ hours weekly switching between email, Slack, and project management tools to track client requests (QuicklyHire, 2024). Priya lives this.

## Core Pain
Projects start clean and turn chaotic. Clients add features mid-build ("can we also add a blog?"), give feedback weeks late, then want the deadline to stay the same. She doesn't have a good system for documenting scope changes, so she either eats the cost or has an awkward conversation. And invoices — freelancers lose an average of $5,000 annually to late or missing payments (TopNotepad).

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Documenting scope changes before doing the work (she usually just does it)
- Following up on late client feedback that's blocking progress
- Sending invoices at project milestones (she often sends at end, loses leverage)
- Following up on unpaid invoices without killing the relationship
- Writing project proposals (she underbids because scoping takes forever)
- Onboarding new clients with a consistent intake process

## How Navi Helps
Navi is her client ops layer — it drafts scope change confirmations, follows up on outstanding feedback, sends milestone invoices, and handles the awkward payment follow-up with the right tone so Priya doesn't have to.

The Flores site has an outstanding invoice ($2,800, 11 days past due). Want me to send a nudge for both? Also: the Kowalski proposal is due Friday and you haven't started it.
