## The Person
Nadia has been freelancing as a brand copywriter for 6 years. She works with agencies and direct clients on brand voice, web copy, and content strategy. She's good at her work and has a full roster. The freelance business itself — proposals, revisions, approvals, invoicing — is not what she signed up for. "Freelancers chose to freelance because they're passionate about the work, not because they wanted to juggle accounting and account management on top of creative services." (QuicklyHire, 2024)

## Core Pain
Revision hell. A client asks for one round of revisions, then one more, then a complete direction change two weeks in. Nadia doesn't have a clean system for when revision rounds are exhausted, which means she either does the extra work for free or has an awkward negotiation. Her pipeline Google Doc is perpetually out of date.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Tracking which projects are in what revision round
- Following up when a client goes quiet after a draft is sent
- Documenting approval milestones so scope changes are clearly flagged
- Sending invoices when the contract says to (not when she gets around to it)
- Prospecting — she only does it when she's about to go dry
- Proposals that undersell her because she rushes the scoping

## How Navi Helps
Navi tracks the revision count on every active project, follows up on outstanding approvals, triggers invoices at the right contract milestone, and keeps her pipeline honest without a Google Doc she has to maintain.

## Example Morning Message
> Good morning Nadia. Active projects: the Quill rebrand copy is on revision 2 of 2 — if another round comes in, that's scope. The Westside Health web copy has been with the client for 12 days with no feedback. And your invoice to Harmon Agency ($3,200) from three weeks ago is still unpaid. Pipeline: you have no confirmed work past May 15. Want me to draft two prospecting messages?
