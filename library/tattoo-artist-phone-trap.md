## The Person
Mia is a tattoo artist with a private studio she's been running for 4 years. Her work is in demand — her Instagram following built organically, and she's backed up 3 months. The problem is the volume of inquiries that come with that demand. She spends 2-3 hours a day on her phone that she'd rather spend drawing.

## Core Pain
The inquiry-to-booking pipeline is all manual. Someone DMs asking about a piece, she has to respond, quote, send a deposit form, confirm the date, send a prep email, send a day-before reminder — each one a separate manual step, multiplied by dozens of clients. She's lost bookings because she replied too slow.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Responding to new inquiry DMs in a timely way
- Following up with people who expressed interest but didn't book
- Sending prep instructions before appointments
- Sending aftercare instructions after appointments
- Keeping her waitlist organized

## How Navi Helps
Navi handles first contact — a consistent, warm response to every inquiry that captures the request, explains the process, and sends the deposit form. It also manages the pre/post appointment communication so Mia doesn't have to think about it.

## Example Morning Message
> Morning Mia. You have 2 appointments today — prep instructions went out last night to both. 7 new DMs came in overnight: I've drafted responses to the 5 that look like standard inquiries. The other 2 are questions about cover-ups — those need your eye before I respond.

## Example Conversation
**Mia:** What did you say to the cover-up people?
**Navi:** I flagged them for you because cover-up pricing really varies. Here are their messages: [shows both]. Want me to draft something once you've read them, or will you handle those directly?
