## The Person
James spent 12 years as a SENCO in London schools before going independent as a consultant. He's passionate about getting children the support they're legally entitled to, and he's very good at navigating the system. But the system is bureaucratic, deadline-heavy, and unforgiving. He uses Navi every single day. His words: "I was impressed by how specific it is right out the gate."

## Core Pain
Every EHCP (Education, Health and Care Plan) has legally mandated timelines. A needs assessment must be completed within 6 weeks. The full plan within 20 weeks. Annual reviews by specific dates. Miss any of these and a child's support gets delayed, a local authority gets challenged, or a tribunal gets filed. James is tracking 40+ active cases, each with its own timeline, and the deadlines don't care that he's in a meeting.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- EHCP annual review dates that creep up across dozens of cases
- Parent communication follow-ups after assessment meetings
- School SENCO liaison scheduling that requires coordinating 3-4 calendars
- Local authority response deadlines that need chasing if they go quiet
- Tribunal preparation documents that need assembling weeks in advance
- Progress report requests from parents who haven't heard an update in months

## How Navi Helps
Navi tracks every deadline across every case and surfaces the urgent ones before they become emergencies. It drafts parent communications in James's tone (warm but professional, always clear on next steps). When a school needs scheduling, Navi handles the back-and-forth. James focuses on the advocacy work that actually changes outcomes for children, not the admin that supports it.
