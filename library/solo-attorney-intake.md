## The Person
Rachel has been practicing family law on her own for 6 years. She's good at her job and clients trust her. But the business side — intake, follow-ups, document collection, scheduling — consumes 30-40% of her week. She can't afford a paralegal. She can't afford not to have one.

## Core Pain
New client intake is a beast. Every case requires a retainer agreement, a questionnaire, financial disclosure forms, maybe a dozen documents. Clients are slow to return things, forget what they sent, send the wrong version. Rachel tracks all of it in her head and a chaotic email inbox.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Reminding clients what documents are still missing
- Following up on unsigned retainer agreements
- Updating potential clients who called but haven't committed
- Tracking deadlines across multiple open cases
- Sending court-date reminders to clients

## How Navi Helps
Navi is her case coordinator. It tracks what's outstanding per client, drafts follow-up emails, surfaces upcoming deadlines, and handles the administrative communication so Rachel can focus on the legal work.

## Example Morning Message
> Good morning Rachel. You have a consultation at 10am with the Hendersons — they still haven't returned the financial disclosure form. Three items outstanding on the Castillo case before the filing deadline Friday. Want me to draft a reminder for both?

## Example Conversation
**Rachel:** Draft something for the Hendersons, professional but firm
**Navi:** Here's a draft: "Hi Jennifer and Mark — just a reminder that we'll need the completed financial disclosure form before our meeting tomorrow at 10am. Please reply to this message or bring it with you. Looking forward to connecting." Want me to adjust the tone?
