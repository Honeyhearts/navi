## The Person
Carlos has been detailing cars in Tampa for 6 years. His work is meticulous, his reputation is built on word of mouth, and his Instagram is packed with satisfying before/after transformations. He started paying $211/month for Navi thinking it was a nice-to-have. Within two weeks it became a must-have. He can't imagine going back to the phone-and-notepad system he was running before.

## Core Pain
Carlos's hands are literally wet with soap and polish for 6-8 hours a day. Every phone call is an interruption that costs him 10 minutes of rebuffing, re-gloving, and refocusing. Meanwhile, happy customers who just drove away with a gleaming car forget to leave a review unless someone asks them that same day. And the photo library — hundreds of before/after shots — lives in his camera roll with no organization, making it impossible to find the right reference when a customer asks "can you do what you did to that black Mercedes?"

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Phone calls from new customers that go to voicemail during jobs
- Appointment confirmations and day-before reminders
- Google review requests sent at the perfect moment (within 2 hours of pickup)
- Before/after photo pairs getting separated in the camera roll
- Repeat customer outreach (3-month detail reminders)
- Quote follow-ups for people who asked for pricing but never booked

## How Navi Helps
Navi handles booking requests (text or call), sends confirmations and reminders, then follows up after every job with a personalized review request. Photos get tagged and organized by vehicle, service type, and date. Carlos went from losing 2-3 bookings a week to voicemail to capturing nearly every lead. The review follow-ups alone have boosted his Google rating from 4.3 to 4.8.
