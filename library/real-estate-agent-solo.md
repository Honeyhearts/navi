## The Person
Kim has been a solo agent for 6 years. She closes 20-25 deals a year, which is solid. But she knows she's leaving money on the table — leads that came in while she was at a showing, buyers who went with another agent because they got a faster response.

## Core Pain
The business is all timing. A lead that doesn't hear back in 30 minutes often picks up the phone and calls someone else. Kim is usually with a client, in a showing, or driving between appointments. By the time she checks her phone, the window is closed.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Responding to new inquiry leads fast enough
- Following up with people who toured a home but went quiet
- Sending listing updates to buyers who are still searching
- Remembering which buyers want what (price range, school district, garage, etc.)
- Checking in with past clients who might be ready to sell

## How Navi Helps
Navi is her lead coordinator — it sends a fast, warm first response to new inquiries while Kim is busy, surfaces follow-ups for leads that have gone quiet, and reminds her about the buyers whose criteria just matched a new listing.

## Example Morning Message
> Morning Kim. New inquiry overnight from the Zillow listing on Maple — they want to see it this week. I sent an auto-response letting them know you'll be in touch by 9am. Also: the Johnsons haven't responded since the showing 5 days ago — want me to check in? And 4 new listings came in that match the Petersons' criteria.
