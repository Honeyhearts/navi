## The Person
Amber has operated a home grooming studio for 5 years. She grooms 4-5 dogs a day with 2-3 hour slots. She's excellent at her work — her regulars rebook immediately and she has a waitlist. A single no-show wastes an entire slot that can't be refilled on short notice. She manages everything through texts and a paper appointment book, which is charming until it costs her $150 for a two-hour window she can't fill.

## Core Pain
No-shows and late arrivals. A dog who shows up 45 minutes late pushes her whole day. She sends reminder texts when she remembers, which isn't always. She also struggles to manage her waitlist — she has a list of names in her phone contacts but no system for knowing who's next or how to fill a cancellation slot on short notice.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Sending appointment reminders 48 hours and morning-of
- Filling cancellation slots from the waitlist quickly
- Following up when a regular is overdue for their next groom (most dogs need grooming every 6-8 weeks)
- Asking satisfied clients for Google or Yelp reviews
- Handling new client intake (vaccination records, breed, temperament notes) before the appointment

## How Navi Helps
Navi runs her booking operations — reminders go out automatically, cancellations trigger a waitlist notification, and overdue regulars get a nudge before someone else books the slot they usually hold.

## Example Morning Message
> Good morning Amber. Today: 4 appointments starting at 8am. All 4 confirmed. One thing: Bella (Goldendoodle, the Petersons) is 9 weeks out from her last groom — they're your most consistent client, usually book every 7 weeks. Want me to reach out? Also: you have 6 people on the waitlist — if you get a cancellation today, want me to text the top 2 automatically?

## Example Conversation
**Amber:** Yes auto-text the first two on the waitlist if I get a cancellation
**Navi:** Set. If a cancellation comes in I'll text [Waitlist 1] and [Waitlist 2] immediately with the open slot. First to respond gets it. I'll let you know who books.
