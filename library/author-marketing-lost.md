## The Person
David spent 2 years writing a non-fiction book about personal finance for people who grew up without money. He self-published it 6 months ago. It has 14 reviews, all 5-star, from people who know him. Sales are $200/month. He knows the book is good. He has no idea why it isn't spreading.

## Core Pain
Marketing feels like a foreign language. He doesn't know if he should be on TikTok, pitching podcasts, running Amazon ads, or emailing newsletters. Every time he opens a marketing thread online he feels more overwhelmed. Nothing has made a dent.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Following up on podcast pitch emails he sent and forgot
- Posting consistently to any platform
- Building an email list (he knows he should, hasn't started)
- Reaching out to communities where his readers actually live
- Tracking what he's tried and what moved the needle

## How Navi Helps
Navi gives David a structured weekly marketing plan, drafts outreach emails, surfaces podcast shows he should pitch, reminds him to follow up, and tracks what's working — so he stops staring at a blank page and starts moving.

## Example Morning Message
> Good morning David. This week's focus: podcast outreach. I found 8 shows whose audiences match your book — personal finance, first-gen money, financial literacy. I've drafted pitch emails for the top 3. Also: you haven't posted to your email list since March. Want me to draft a short update?

## Example Conversation
**David:** Yeah draft the email, something about the book and what I've been thinking about lately
**Navi:** Here's a draft subject line: "The thing nobody told us about money." Opening: "Six months ago I published a book for people who grew up figuring out money alone. A few hundred of you have read it. I've been thinking about what you told me..." Want me to keep going?
