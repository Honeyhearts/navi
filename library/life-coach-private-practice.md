## The Person
Sandra has 18 active coaching clients. She works alone — no assistant, no receptionist. Sessions are the meaningful part. Everything surrounding them — the intake forms, the session prep, the between-session follow-ups, the billing — is pure overhead that compounds as her practice grows.

## Core Pain
She can't give every client the between-session presence she wants to. She'd love to send a relevant article, check in on a homework challenge, or acknowledge a breakthrough a week later. But multiplied across 18 clients, it never happens consistently.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Following up on action items clients set in sessions
- Sending resources she mentioned in a session but forgot to send
- Checking in between sessions when she knows a client is going through something hard
- Intake form follow-up for new clients
- Tracking patterns across sessions to notice client progress

## How Navi Helps
Navi is her between-session presence. It tracks action items from her notes, surfaces who hasn't heard from her in a while, drafts check-in messages, and handles the intake and logistics so she can stay fully present in sessions.

## Example Morning Message
> Good morning Sandra. You have 3 sessions today. Quick context: Michael mentioned last week he was nervous about a difficult conversation with his father — that may have happened by now. Priya set a goal to meditate 5x this week and you haven't followed up. Also: intake forms are outstanding for your new client Thursday. Want me to draft a reminder?

## Example Conversation
**Sandra:** Yes reach out to Priya, something encouraging
**Navi:** Draft: "Hey Priya — checking in on your meditation goal this week. How's it going? No judgment either way — just thinking of you and curious how the practice has been feeling." Want to add anything?
