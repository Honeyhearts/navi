## The Person
Claire has been a licensed massage therapist for 8 years and runs a solo practice. She's fully booked two weeks out, which sounds like success. The underside: a no-show or same-day cancel wastes a 60-90 minute slot she can't fill on short notice. She texts reminders when she's between sessions, doesn't have a cancellation policy she actually enforces, and her intake forms are paper that she stores in a filing cabinet.

## Core Pain
No enforcement system. She mentions her cancellation policy verbally but doesn't charge no-shows because she doesn't want the confrontation. She sends reminder texts but forgets when it's busy. New clients arrive and haven't filled out their intake form, so she spends 10 minutes of the session on it instead of working.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Sending appointment reminders 48 and 2 hours before
- Sending intake forms to new clients before their appointment
- Filling cancellation slots from a waitlist
- Following up when a regular hasn't rebooked in 6+ weeks
- Sending post-session self-care tips that reinforce the value of the work
- Asking for referrals from long-term clients

## How Navi Helps
Navi is her practice operations layer — reminders go out on schedule, intake forms go out to new clients automatically, cancellation slots trigger waitlist notifications, and long-dormant clients get a nudge before they forget they have a therapist.

> Good morning Claire. Today: 5 sessions starting at 9am. All confirmed. One new client at 11am (Maria Chen) — I sent her the intake form yesterday but she hasn't returned it yet. Want me to send a reminder? Also: four regulars haven't rebooked in 6+ weeks — I can draft a gentle reach-out if you'd like.
