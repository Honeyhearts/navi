## The Person
Beth has run her Etsy shop for 4 years. During the holidays she's shipping 80-100 orders a week and fielding 30+ customer messages. The rest of the year it's manageable. November and December nearly break her every time.

## Core Pain
Customer messages pile up. Most are the same 5 questions — "where's my order," "can I customize this," "what's your return policy." She answers them manually, every time, while also packaging, labeling, and running to the post office.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Responding to customer messages fast enough to maintain her response time rating
- Noticing when a bestseller is about to run out of stock
- Tracking delayed shipments before customers ask about them
- Following up with wholesale inquiries that went quiet
- Asking for reviews after delivery

## How Navi Helps
Navi handles first-response customer messages, surfaces low-stock warnings, tracks shipping delays, and sends post-delivery review requests — so Beth can stay in the studio making things.

## Example Morning Message
> Morning Beth. 14 new messages overnight — 9 are order status questions, I've drafted responses for all 9 using their tracking info. 3 are customization requests that need your input. Balsam & Cedar is down to 12 units — you typically reorder at 15. And 7 delivered orders from last week haven't left reviews yet. Want me to send a gentle ask?
