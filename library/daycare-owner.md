## The Person
Theresa has run a licensed home daycare for 6 years. She cares for 8 children ages 6 months to 4 years. She is fully certified, deeply caring, and constantly overwhelmed by the administrative layer. Research shows that 50% of child care centers still communicate with parents using paper (Brightwheel, 2024) — Theresa uses a combination of paper, texts, and a Facebook group that nobody checks.

## Core Pain
Billing is a nightmare. Some parents pay on time, some are always late, and Theresa hates asking for money from the families she cares for. Sending daily updates to 8 families takes 30 minutes she doesn't have. And every time a child has an incident or a milestone, she has to document it while also managing 7 other children.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Billing reminders for late-paying parents (she avoids this conversation)
- Daily communication updates to all families
- Waitlist follow-up for families who inquired months ago
- Monthly newsletters and policy reminders
- Tracking immunization and enrollment paperwork deadlines
- Documenting incidents while managing the room

## How Navi Helps
Navi handles the parent communication layer — billing reminders, daily summary drafts, waitlist follow-ups — so Theresa can put her phone down and be fully present with the children.

## Example Morning Message
> Good morning Theresa. Two billing reminders: the Chen family is 5 days past the 1st — $890 outstanding. The Williams family is 12 days past. Want me to send a gentle reminder to both? Also: three families on your waitlist haven't heard from you in 6+ weeks — I can draft a quick check-in. And you have a new inquiry from a family looking for full-time care starting in May.
