## The Person
Gwen has run a specialty plant nursery for 7 years. She knows plants deeply — the seasonality, the soil conditions, which varieties her regulars come back for every spring. What she doesn't know is how to run a business that has two completely different operating modes: spring chaos and winter planning. She handles both badly because there's no system that accounts for the shift.

## Core Pain
Spring arrives and everything breaks at once. Consultations fill up, her regulars want to know what's new, she needs to push seasonal content, and new inventory comes in faster than she can tag and announce it. She makes it through every spring on willpower and loses customers who couldn't get a response in time.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Reminding customers when it's time to plant what they bought last year
- Announcing new arrivals before they sell out without anyone knowing
- Managing the consultation waitlist during peak season
- Adjusting communications from "planning mode" (winter) to "chaos mode" (spring) automatically
- Following up with wholesale clients who come in once a year
- Capturing contact info from walk-in customers for future outreach

## How Navi Helps
Navi runs the seasonal calendar — it knows when spring transitions are coming, adjusts the communication cadence automatically, surfaces the customers due for planting reminders, and handles new arrival announcements so Gwen can spend that time actually working with the plants.
