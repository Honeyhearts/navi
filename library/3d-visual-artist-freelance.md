## The Person
Leo is a freelance 3D artist with 6 years of experience in motion graphics, product visualization, and AI-assisted image pipelines. He's technically deep — comfortable in Blender, ComfyUI, Houdini. When he's working, he's truly working: 4-hour flow states where nothing exists except the scene in front of him. This is both his superpower and his problem.

## Core Pain
He surfaces from a render session at 4pm to find three client messages, a revision request from a project he thought was closed, and a deadline he'd mentally moved but hadn't confirmed with the client. He doesn't have "leads" or a sales pipeline — he has production cycles, and the business stuff intrudes on the work in the worst possible way.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Responding to client check-ins while he's in a flow state
- Tracking which projects are in what stage (draft, client review, revision, final delivery)
- Flagging when a render deadline is approaching and a client hasn't responded to approve the draft
- Sending delivery emails when files are ready
- Following up on invoices after project close
- Managing revision requests across multiple concurrent projects

## How Navi Helps
Navi is his production coordinator that operates while he creates. It tracks project statuses, drafts client updates, flags when he needs to surface from a session to handle something time-sensitive, and handles the post-delivery business side so Leo can close out a project cleanly and move to the next one.

## Example Morning Message
> Good morning Leo. Production check: the Müller product viz is in client review — they've had it 4 days with no response, deadline is Friday. The Haze Studios motion piece has a revision request from yesterday — two notes, both minor. And the February invoice for the Koller project is 12 days unpaid. Want me to draft a nudge?
