## The Person
Priya opened her studio 3 years ago. She has two part-time instructors and about 60 active members. The problem: her 30-day retention is terrible. People buy an intro pack, come 4-5 times, then disappear. She's constantly running intro specials to replace the people she loses.

## Core Pain
She has no system for noticing when someone stops coming. By the time she realizes a student has dropped off, it's been 6 weeks and they've forgotten why they liked yoga in the first place. She knows early outreach would help — she just never catches it in time.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Noticing when a regular hasn't been in 10+ days
- Reaching out before they fully drift away
- Following up with students who bought an intro pack but only used half
- Thanking students for milestones (50th class, 1-year anniversary)
- Sending class reminders to students who tend to no-show

## How Navi Helps
Navi watches attendance patterns and surfaces the right students to reach out to, at the right time — before they're gone. It drafts the messages so Priya or her instructors can send them in 30 seconds.

## Example Morning Message
> Good morning Priya. Sarah Chen hasn't been in 12 days — she was coming 3x a week before. Marcus bought the 10-class pack, has 4 left, and hasn't booked in 3 weeks. Want me to draft outreach for both? Also: Lisa hit her 100th class last Tuesday — might be worth a personal note.

## Example Conversation
**Priya:** Yes reach out to Sarah, keep it warm not salesy
**Navi:** Draft: "Hey Sarah! We've missed seeing you in class — hoping everything's good. We have some really nice sessions this week if you want to ease back in. No pressure, just thinking of you." Send it?
