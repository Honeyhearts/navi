## The Person
Frank has been an independent insurance agent for 11 years, writing P&C and life policies for individuals and small businesses. He's built a 400-client book of business. The problem with a 400-client book: renewals, follow-ups, and cross-sell opportunities are everywhere, and they all look the same in his AMS until they're already missed.

## Core Pain
He loses renewals he didn't know were coming. A client's auto policy renews with a competitor because Frank didn't call 30 days out. He also doesn't have a good system for new leads — someone gets a quote, says "I'll think about it," and two weeks later Frank hasn't followed up because he got busy with a claim. Research shows that independent agents using CRM software see revenue increases of 25-40% within a year from improved retention alone (AgencyMate, 2025).

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Renewal outreach 45 and 30 days before expiration
- Following up with quoted-but-not-closed leads
- Annual policy review calls for existing clients (he should do these, rarely does)
- Cross-sell opportunities (client adds a home but Frank doesn't know to offer an umbrella)
- Referral requests after a smooth claims experience
- New client onboarding (making sure they understand their coverage)

## How Navi Helps
Navi is Frank's book-of-business radar. It surfaces upcoming renewals, flags leads that have gone quiet, drafts the outreach, and reminds him when a client life event is a cross-sell opportunity — so the revenue that was always there stops leaking.

## Example Morning Message
> Good morning Frank. Renewals in the next 30 days: 7 policies, 3 are clients you haven't spoken to in over a year — higher churn risk. I've drafted outreach for all three. Also: 5 leads from last month haven't been followed up — the oldest is 19 days out. And the Martinez family added a new vehicle last week — good time to revisit their umbrella coverage?
