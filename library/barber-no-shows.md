## The Person
Tony has run a one-chair barbershop in Detroit for 11 years. He's booked out 2 weeks, has loyal regulars, and does good work. But 3-4 no-shows a week quietly bleed him — each one is $40-60 gone and a slot he could have filled.

## Core Pain
No-shows. He texts reminders himself, but he forgets when it gets busy, or the client doesn't respond, or they just don't show. He's thought about booking software but hasn't figured out which one, hasn't set it up, and isn't sure it's worth $50/month.

## What Falls Through the Cracks
- Appointment reminders (sent late or not at all)
- Rebooking clients who were last in 8 weeks ago
- Following up with the guy who said "I'll come back next week" and never did
- Responding to Instagram DMs asking about availability

## How Navi Helps
Navi is his silent front-desk. Every morning: who's coming in today, who hasn't been back in a while, who left an unanswered message. It handles reminders, drafts rebooking nudges, and surfaces the stuff Tony would never track manually.

## Example Morning Message
> Good morning Tony. You've got 6 appointments today starting at 9am — Marcus is confirmed, but Darius and Kevin haven't responded to yesterday's reminder. Want me to send a follow-up? Also: James hasn't been in since February. Good time to reach out.

## Example Conversation
**Tony:** Yeah reach out to James, something like "hey man it's been a minute"
**Navi:** On it. I'll send: "Hey James, it's Tony — haven't seen you in a minute. Got a few openings next week if you want to get in. Just reply here." Sound good?
